FAQ

Didn't find your answer? Call +421 902 464 022 or email apoliak@apoliak.online.

What happens after I get in touch?

Within 24 hours I'll reply or call and ask about anything missing from your message. Then you get the scope, price and date in writing. If we agree, you'll have the first design within three days. Until you order the site, you pay nothing.

Can I have a site like one of the examples?

Yes. Tell me which demo you like and what you do. I'll prepare a design for your business, with your name, services and colours.

What does the free consultation involve?

We go through what your business needs. I suggest a scope, give you an estimate of price and timing, and explain what each package includes. We do it by phone or email. There's no obligation, and you get my recommendation even if you don't hire me.

What will I pay after the first year?

You don't pay monthly for the site itself. You only pay for the domain and hosting, directly to the provider. Maintenance from €9 a month is optional; the site runs without it.

Who will update my daily menu or price list?

We agree on it up front. Either I set the site up so you can do it yourself, or I do it as part of maintenance. It'll be written in the quote.

Do I have to supply the texts and photos?

No. If you have copy, I'll use it. If not, I'll write it: it's included in the Pro package, otherwise it costs €19 per page. If you don't have photos, I'll build the design so it doesn't need them.

What if I don't like the design?

You tell me what to change and I rework it. Starter includes one round of changes, Standard two, and with Pro we handle them as we go.

Who owns the website and the domain?

You. The domain is registered in your name, and the site and source code are yours once paid for. If you want to move to someone else, you can hand everything over without my permission.

Why are you cheaper than agencies?

I do everything myself, so you don't pay for an agency's overheads. I build without paid templates or plugins, so there are no monthly licences either. The typical market price is shown next to each package in the price list.

Will people find my site on Google?

Basic SEO is included in every package: filled-in descriptions, proper headings, a sitemap and fast loading. I don't promise first place in the results; Google sets the order by its own rules.

Tell me what you need

A few sentences about what you do and what the site should handle are enough. You don't need to know which package you want. I'll get back to you within 24 hours with a price and a date.
