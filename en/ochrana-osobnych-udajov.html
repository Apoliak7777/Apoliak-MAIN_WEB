Privacy policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Who processes your data

The controller is Apoliak (apoliak.online), a sole trader based in the Bratislava Region, Slovakia, Business ID (IČO) 57 990 883, registered in the Trade Register of the Senec District Office under No. 140-39176. For anything to do with personal data, contact me at apoliak@apoliak.online or on +421 902 464 022. Technical support for finished websites is handled at support@apoliak.online.

What data I process and why

  • Your enquiry and quote: the data you send me by email, phone, text message or WhatsApp (name, email, phone number and the content of the message). Legal basis: steps taken at your request before entering into a contract and my legitimate interest in replying to you (Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR). If we don't end up working together, I delete the messages within 12 months at the latest.
  • Building the website and invoicing: contact and billing details and the materials for the website. Legal basis: performance of a contract and a legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(b) and (c) GDPR). I keep them for the duration of our work together, and invoices and related documents for as long as tax law requires (up to 10 years).
  • Contacting businesses by email: business email address, company name and public information about the company (such as its industry, registered office and size). Legal basis: my legitimate interest in offering websites to businesses (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). I keep them for no more than 2 years after the last contact.

Providing your data is voluntary, but without your contact details I can't reply to you or build your website.

Contacting businesses by email

I email companies and sole traders with an offer of a website. I get the business email address and company name from the company's website or from public registers and business databases. I decide the order in which I contact companies based on public information about them, such as industry and size. This has no legal effects for you.

You can object to being contacted at any time: just reply in one sentence and I won't contact you again with any of my offers. I'll keep only your address so that I don't write to you again.

Who I share data with

I don't sell data. Only the providers of the services I use for this have access to it: Hostinger International Ltd. (Cyprus) runs my mailbox, GitHub, Inc. (USA) hosts this website and logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes, and WhatsApp messages are delivered by WhatsApp Ireland Limited (Meta group). Transfers to the USA are based on the European Commission's adequacy decision (EU-US Data Privacy Framework).

Cookies and analytics

This site uses no cookies and no tracking or analytics tools.

Email from the buttons on the site

The home page has no contact form. The email buttons open your email program with a drafted message, which you then send me from your own address. Nothing is sent through this site and no third party gets access to the message.

Messages via WhatsApp

If you message me on WhatsApp, the message goes through Meta's WhatsApp service, which processes it under its own terms. I use it the same way as an email — only to reply to your enquiry. If you would rather not, send me an email or give me a call.

Example websites

The site contains example websites in the /ukazky/ folder. They are designs for made-up businesses, not websites of real clients. They collect no data — the bookings, orders, calculators and shopping cart in them work only in your browser, and nothing is sent or stored anywhere.

Your rights

You have the right to access your data, to have it corrected or erased, to restrict its processing and to data portability, and the right to object to processing based on legitimate interest, including being contacted by email. Just write to apoliak@apoliak.online and I'll deal with it within one month at the latest.

If you believe I am handling your data unlawfully, you can lodge a complaint with the Slovak data protection authority (Office for Personal Data Protection of the Slovak Republic), Hraničná 12, 820 07 Bratislava 27, dataprotection.gov.sk, or with the supervisory authority in the country where you live or work.

These are the basic principles of how I protect personal data. If you have any questions, feel free to contact me.
