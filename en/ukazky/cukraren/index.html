About the bakery

A small bakery where everything is baked on site

Crust is a bakery with a pastry kitchen in one room behind the shop. We have no central production unit and no deliveries of frozen part-baked goods — we knead the dough in the evening, load the bread into the oven before dawn and finish the pastries during the morning.

Our flour comes from a mill 40 kilometres away, we use 82% butter and eggs from a farm in the county. Bread gets flour, water, salt and sourdough. Nothing else belongs in it, so you won't find improvers or preservatives in the ingredients.

Anything left over is sold at half price in the last hour before closing. Day-old bread costs €2.20 in the morning, whatever the size of the loaf.

FAQ

What people ask most often

How far ahead should I order a cake?

A simple cake up to 22 cm needs three working days. Larger sizes need four, a two-tier cake six. A marzipan covering adds a day, a hand-painted finish two days. The builder shows the exact earliest date.

Do you make gluten-free or lactose-free products?

Yes — we have a gluten-free almond slice every day, and a gluten-free cake sponge for an extra €12. We can make a lactose-free cake with plant-based cream by arrangement. The kitchen isn't separate, though, so with coeliac disease we can't rule out traces of gluten.

Can you make a cake from my own design?

Send us a picture or a description. We'll adapt the colour and theme, but we don't promise to copy someone else's work in every detail. We price more complex decoration individually, always in advance and with your agreement.

How long does sourdough bread keep?

Three to four days. Store it cut side down in a cloth bag or a bread bin, not in plastic. Don't put it in the fridge — that's where it goes stale fastest.

How long does a finished cake keep?

Two days in the fridge at 4 to 6 °C. We recommend eating cakes with fresh fruit and cream on the day of collection. Leave the cake at room temperature for 30 minutes before serving — it tastes much better.

Do you deliver?

We deliver in town Tuesday to Friday between 9:00 and 11:00 for €5. Delivery is free for orders over €100. We don't deliver outside town — cakes don't travel well.

Can I sit down and have a coffee?

We have two tables for four and a standing counter by the window. Espresso is €1.60 and cappuccino €2.20. Coffee is served Tuesday to Friday until 16:30 and on Saturday until 12:00. We don't take table reservations.

Do you also supply cafés wholesale?

Yes — for at least 30 rolls a day on a fixed schedule, prices are 20% below shop prices. You'll find the full terms, including capacity, in the Wholesale and catering section.
