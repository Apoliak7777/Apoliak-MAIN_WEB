Emergency service

What to do until we arrive

Call us and then do this — you'll save yourself damage and money. We'll also talk you through it on the phone while we get into the van.

within 60 minutes

Burst pipe

Turn off the main stopcock — it's usually under the kitchen sink or where the pipe comes into the house. If water is leaking into the flat below, tell your neighbours straight away, not after the repair.

within 60 minutes

Boiler down

Try resetting it once with the reset button and take a photo of the error code on the display. If it cuts out again, don't keep restarting it — with a burner fault that does damage.

immediately

You smell gas

Don't switch on lights or anything electrical, open the windows, turn off the gas at the meter and go outside. Call the Gas Networks Ireland emergency line on 1800 20 50 50, and then us.

within 90 minutes

Blocked drain

Don't pour chemical drain cleaners in — with older cast-iron pipes they do more harm than good. We'll bring a drain snake and a camera if needed.

within 90 minutes

No hot water

If you have a cylinder with an immersion, check whether the breaker or thermostat has tripped. With a combi boiler, the fault is usually in the diverter valve — and that's a job for us.

within 24 hours

Cold radiators

Try bleeding them and topping up the system pressure to 1.5 bar. If the pressure drops every week, water is escaping somewhere, and it needs finding before it soaks through a ceiling.

FAQ

What you ask us most often

Is a heat pump worth it in an older house?

It depends on what water temperature your radiators need. In an uninsulated house with radiators at 70 °C, a heat pump won't save much and tops up with electricity in frosty weather. If you're insulating the house or putting in underfloor heating, it's a completely different story. We'll work it out for your house — and if it doesn't add up, we'll tell you.

How long will I be without water when the pipes are replaced?

In an apartment usually one working day, two for a complete riser replacement. We agree when to turn it off in advance and can give your neighbours a notice for their door. We always leave the water working overnight if it's technically possible.

Do I need to sort out the chimney when I replace the boiler?

When switching to a condensing boiler, almost always — the flue gases are cooler and the old chimney can't handle them. It's done by fitting a plastic flue liner. We can arrange that along with a chimney inspection, so you don't need to find another company.

Do you do tiling and sanitaryware too, or just the pipework?

We do turnkey bathrooms, including tiling, sanitaryware and ventilation. The only thing we don't do is the electrics — for that we have a trusted electrician who comes at a time we agree between ourselves, so you don't have to.

What is an annual boiler service, and is it required?

A leak check, cleaning the burner and heat exchanger, checking the pressure and adjusting the combustion. It isn't required by law for an ordinary house, but manufacturers make their warranty depend on it, and without it the boiler burns more gas. We also service boilers we didn't install.

What's the guarantee?

Seven years on installation; equipment we supply carries the manufacturer's warranty, usually two to five years depending on the brand. If something fails, we come within 48 hours, and if it's our fault, we fix it free of charge, call-out included.
