The day

A day at the gym, hour by hour

How a normal weekday looks. At weekends the gym is busiest between 09:00 and 12:00, when the group classes are on.

  1. 05:00We open. Reception, changing rooms and the sauna are running from the first minute.
  2. 06:00Morning Strength on Monday and Wednesday, and on Friday at 06:30. On Tuesday and Thursday the timetable starts with mobility at 07:00.
  3. 09:00The quietest hours of the day. There are up to twenty people in the gym and the racks are free.
  4. 12:00Lunchtime rush. The treadmills and rowers are the busiest.
  5. 16:30The afternoon block of group classes starts in the studio and the functional zone.
  6. 17:00The busiest time of the day. On Monday and Wednesday you might wait ten minutes for a rack.
  7. 19:30The gym empties out; no waiting for racks or benches.
  8. 21:00Sauna and ice bath with no waiting. The quiet room is free.
  9. 22:15Last entry to the gym. The sauna switches off at 22:30.
  10. 23:00We close. Please leave the changing rooms by closing time.

Getting here

Getting here

The gym is at 7 Hall Street in Dublin; the entrance is from the street and has the same sign as the header of this page.

By car

Car park in front of the gym

The entrance is straight off Hall Street. The car park has 40 spaces and is free while you train; two spaces by the entrance are reserved for Disabled Parking Permit holders. After 17:00 the car park is usually full; there's unrestricted parking on the side street.

By bus

Bus stop 200 metres from the entrance

It's three minutes' walk from the stop along the footpath, without crossing a road. The last buses leave after 23:00, so you can make an evening session.

By bike

Covered bike stand

A lockable stand for 20 bikes is right by the entrance under cover and in view of a camera. The changing room showers are available with a single visit too.

Step-free access

Everything on the ground floor

There's no step at the entrance, and the doorway is 90 cm wide. An accessible changing room and toilet are by reception, and there are no thresholds between the zones. Some machines have height-adjustable seats; a trainer will help you set them.

Your first visit

Come 15 minutes early

Sign in at reception, get a locker key and go over the layout of the gym with a trainer. For a group class, go into the studio five minutes before it starts.

What to bring

Trainers, towel, bottle

Trainers with clean soles are a condition of entry to the gym. A towel for the machines is required; we hire them for €1. You don't need a padlock — the lockers have keys.

FAQ

FAQ

These are the questions we get asked most at reception. If you can't find the answer here, write to us using the form below.

Do I need to book group classes?

Yes. Classes take 8 to 20 people depending on the class, and booking opens seven days ahead. You don't need to book the gym floor outside group classes — just come in.

I'm a complete beginner. Can I manage a group class?

Yes. For every exercise the trainer shows an easier and a harder version, and you choose the load yourself. If you're not sure, start with the classes marked Mobility or Conditioning, or book a trial session with a trainer.

Can I train without a membership?

You can. A single visit costs €7, and an early visit before 9:00 on weekdays €5. A group class on top of a single visit is €3 extra. A ten-visit pass is valid for six months and is transferable.

When is the gym quietest?

On weekdays between 09:00 and 14:00, and then after 21:00. The busiest times are Monday and Wednesday between 17:00 and 19:30. At weekends the gym is quiet all day, apart from the morning group classes.

Is the sauna included?

Yes, the sauna and the ice bath are included in every visit and every membership. The sauna switches off half an hour before closing, so at 22:30 on weekdays. Swimwear is required, and so is a towel to sit on.

Can I pause or cancel my membership?

Membership has no contract and doesn't renew automatically, so there's nothing to cancel — you simply don't renew it. During illness or holidays it can be paused for up to 30 days in a calendar year.

Can I bring my child?

Entry to the gym is from age 15, and we don't have a crèche. From 15 to 18, written consent from a parent or guardian is needed; you'll get the form at reception.

Do you do personal training and plans?

Yes. Personal training costs €28 an hour, or €240 for a bundle of ten hours. An initial assessment with a written eight-week plan costs €35 and is included with annual membership. Book at reception or through the form.
