Book a time through the form on this page or by phone. Within 24 hours we'll call you with two free slots and the details for paying the €30 deposit. Your booking is confirmed once the deposit arrives.

Studio and equipment

One hall with a north-facing window and blackout, flash and continuous lights, six paper backdrops and facilities for clients. A viewing is free by arrangement and takes about 15 minutes.

Light

Backdrops and equipment

Facilities

  • Changing room with a mirror and a make-up table

  • Waiting room with Wi-Fi and a baby-changing counter

13 — Terms

Terms and cancellations

The terms are short and apply equally to both sides. You also get them with your booking confirmation, so you have them to hand.

Booking and deposit

Your booking is confirmed once the €30 deposit is paid. The deposit is deducted from the final price; you pay the balance when you collect your photos. We don't take a deposit on business orders paid by invoice.

Cancelling and moving an appointment

Cancelling or moving a booking more than 48 hours before the session is free; the deposit moves to the new date or we refund it within 5 working days. If you cancel less than 48 hours before, the deposit is kept.

Running late

The shoot starts at the agreed time. Arriving late shortens your session, because another booking follows yours. If you're more than 30 minutes late, the booking lapses and the deposit is kept.

Illness and a new date

If you're ill, we'll move your session free of charge, even at the last minute. Just call before the shoot starts and we'll agree a new date, usually within two weeks.

What we deliver

We deliver the agreed number of edited photos. We don't hand over unedited files straight from the camera, or shots that didn't pass our technical selection.

Licence and use

Copyright stays with the studio. You get an unlimited licence to use the photos for personal purposes, and with the corporate package also to promote your company, its website and printed materials.

Publishing in our portfolio

We only publish photos with your written consent. Consent is voluntary, doesn't affect the price and you can withdraw it at any time.

Complaints and reshoots

If the work is technically faulty, we'll fix it or reshoot free of charge within 30 days of delivery. A difference in taste when choosing shots isn't grounds for a complaint.

Children and pets

Children are welcome in the studio when accompanied by an adult. We'll photograph a pet by prior arrangement, so we can adjust the backdrop and leave extra time between bookings.

Data protection

We use the details from the form only to arrange your booking and handle your order. We don't share them with third parties, and after the job is done we keep them only as far as needed for our accounts.

17 — FAQ

FAQ

The eight questions we're asked most often. If yours isn't here, write to us through the form and we'll add the answer.

What should I bring to the shoot?

Two or three outfits without big logos or loud patterns. Three days before your session we'll send specific advice on the colours and cuts that look best in our light. The changing room has a mirror, a steamer and a lint roller.

Do I also get the unedited shots straight from the camera?

No. We deliver the selected, edited photos, which we consider the finished work. We don't hand over unedited files. If you need more photos, you can order them at the package price any time within six months of the shoot.

How does choosing the shots work?

Within 5 working days you get a link to a password-protected gallery. Mark your shots with a click and send your selection with one button. You have 14 days to choose. If you can't decide, we'll help with recommendations right in the gallery.

Can I come with a small child or a dog?

Children are welcome in the studio; the waiting room has a play corner and a baby-changing counter. We'll photograph a pet by prior arrangement, so we can choose a suitable backdrop and leave extra time between bookings to clean up.

What if I don't like the result?

You see the shots on the monitor during the shoot, so we agree on the result while you're still in the studio. If the work were technically faulty, we'll fix it or reshoot free of charge within 30 days of delivery.

Do you do passport and ID photos?

Yes, on weekdays from 9:00 to 11:00 with no appointment. Six prints cost €12 and are ready within 20 minutes. We prepare a digital version sized for passports and driving licences the same day.

Can I hire the studio or have a look beforehand?

A viewing by arrangement is free and takes about 15 minutes. Hiring the studio with lights on its own is arranged individually, depending on how much equipment you need and whether our assistant will be there. Write to us through the form and we'll get back to you within 24 hours.

How quickly will I hear about free dates?

We reply to form messages within 24 hours on working days. In the reply you get two specific free slots and the details for paying the deposit. Free slots are usually 7 to 14 days ahead; in November and December, a month earlier.
