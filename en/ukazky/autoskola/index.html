04 — How the course works

From enrolment to full licence in six steps

At two lessons a week the course takes about ten weeks; at one a week, about four months. You can sit the driving test once you've held your learner permit for six months.

Step 01

Enrolment

Fill in the application on this website or at the office. Bring photo ID and an eyesight report for your learner permit. A €150 deposit confirms your place.

1 visit · 20 minutes

Step 02

Classroom theory

32 hours over sixteen sessions of two class units each. The evening and daytime groups meet twice a week, the weekend group on Saturdays. You can catch up on a missed class in another group. Then you sit the Driver Theory Test and apply for your learner permit.

8 weeks

Step 03

Training ground

Your first 6 hours are on an off-road area: moving off, changing gear, reversing, parallel and bay parking, and the turnabout.

6 hours · 3 lessons

Step 04

On-road lessons

35 hours in town, on rural roads and on national roads, covering all 12 EDT lessons. You book times in the calendar, usually two lessons a week.

35 hours · 9 to 12 weeks

Step 05

Test preparation

You do a mock driving test on a test route with a different instructor. If something isn't right, we arrange extra lessons before your test date.

1 week

Step 06

Test and licence

We help you apply for the driving test with the RSA and make sure you're ready for the date. You sit the test in our car, the one you learned in.

After 6 months on your learner permit

06 — Tests

How the tests work

There are two tests: the theory test before your learner permit and the driving test at the end. We help you book both, and you sit the driving test in the car you learned in.

A computer test on the Rules of the Road, road signs and right-of-way situations. You get the result as soon as you finish.

13 — FAQ

What people ask most often

If your question isn't here, call the office or write to us through the form — we reply within 24 hours.

How many hours of lessons are in the category B course?

41 units of practical training. The first 6 are on the training ground and the other 35 on the road, and together they cover all 12 compulsory EDT lessons. One unit lasts 45 minutes; we usually book two back to back.

By when do I have to finish the course?

The course is valid for twelve months from enrolment. If you need longer, we extend it by three months for €30. It can be extended twice.

What happens if I fail the test?

We help you book the first available retest. We charge €35 for the paperwork and providing the car. If you failed on the driving, we recommend two or three extra lessons at €28 each to practise the specific mistakes.

These retest terms apply for six months from your first test; after that, you'll need refresher lessons.

How old do I have to be to sign up?

We can enrol you at the earliest six months before you reach the minimum age for the category — 17 for a car. We'll check the age for your category together when you enrol. If you're under 18, a parent or guardian signs the application.

Can I choose my instructor and lesson times?

Yes. You choose your instructor when you enrol and then book lessons yourself in the calendar. Free slots run from 7:00 to 19:00. For a €40 surcharge we have slots reserved only after 16:00 and at weekends.

A booked lesson must be cancelled at least 24 hours ahead, otherwise it counts as taken.

I learned in an automatic — can I drive a manual?

No. If you pass in an automatic, your licence is restricted to automatic cars. To remove the restriction you need to pass the driving test in a manual; five conversion lessons with us cost €150.

Can I pay for the course in instalments?

Yes, at no extra cost and with no third party. You pay a €150 deposit on enrolment and we split the rest into three instalments — the first when theory starts, the second after the training ground stage, the third before you apply for the driving test. If you pay the full amount on enrolment, we take off €20.

What do I need to bring to my first lesson?

Photo ID (your learner permit, once you have it) and sturdy shoes. If you wear glasses, bring them — you drive in them just as you will in the test. Nothing else is needed; we provide the car and the materials.
