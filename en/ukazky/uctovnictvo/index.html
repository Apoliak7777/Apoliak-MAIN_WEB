14 FAQ

FAQ

Answers to what clients ask before signing.

Can I switch to you mid-year?

Yes. From your previous accountant I need the journal or nominal ledger, the debtor and creditor ledgers, the asset register and payroll records. I check the data and tell you what's open and what needs putting right. I don't charge for this check.

When do I need to send you my paperwork?

Ordinary paperwork by the 10th of the following month, payroll details by the 3rd. With monthly VAT returns the deadline is tighter, and we'll agree it on the first call.

What if a document is missing?

I can usually identify a missing document from the bank account and tell you which one it is. If it can't be found, I'll tell you the effect on tax and VAT before the return is filed.

Who's responsible if there's a penalty?

I'm responsible for mistakes on my side, and I hold professional indemnity insurance. If a penalty arises from paperwork delivered after the agreed date, that's down to the client. That's why I tell you the deadlines in advance and remind you.

What do you do when a letter or an audit notice comes from Revenue?

Forward me the letter and I'll prepare the reply and the paperwork. For an audit, I deal with Revenue directly as your agent, and you only need to be there if necessary. The Standard plan includes three hours and the Extended plan has unlimited representation; outside a plan, the rate is €35 an hour.

Do I need to buy accounting software?

No. I use my own software, and you get the output as PDFs. If you already have an invoicing system, I can import data from it.

Do you do the accounts for online shops and sales abroad?

Yes. I match payments from gateways and cash on delivery, deal with returns, and handle OSS for sales to consumers in other EU countries. With larger volumes we set up automatic import, so the number of documents doesn't push up the fee unnecessarily.

My company isn't trading. Do I have to pay the full fee?

No. I look after a company with no sales and no employees for €25 a month. That includes nil returns, the accounts and filing with the CRO. Once the company starts trading, we move to a normal plan from the following month.

Am I claiming all the expenses I'm entitled to?

I go through your real figures before the return is filed and tell you in euro what each claim is worth. Some claims need more record-keeping, so I factor in the time and cost of processing too.

How do we keep in touch?

You write directly to the accountant who looks after your books, not a general inbox. The form on this page gets a reply within 24 hours on working days, and the phone is answered from 9.00 to 16.00. Queries during the year are included in the monthly fee.
