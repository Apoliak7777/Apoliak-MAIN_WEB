04The house

Around the house

The house has three floors. On the ground floor are reception, the dining room, the lounge, room 01 and both apartments with their own entrances. Rooms 05 – 08 are on the first floor, rooms 02 – 04 in the attic, and the sauna and plant room in the basement.

The whole house is non-smoking. You can smoke under the shelter by the entrance; the ashtray is on the post to the left.

05Food

Breakfast and dinner

Breakfast is included in your stay. We cook dinner from Thursday to Sunday; order by 15:00 on the day.

Breakfast is a buffet in the ground-floor dining room from 7:30 to 10:00, and on the terrace too in summer. If you're setting off early, we'll make you a packed breakfast — just tell reception by 20:00 the day before.

Dinner is served from 18:00 to 20:00 and costs €22 per person, with a choice of meat or meat-free menus. Order by 15:00 on the day.

We can make gluten-free and lactose-free meals — please mention it in your booking. Non-residents are welcome in the dining room too; book a table by phone before 15:00.

We write the menu around what's available that week. Regulars include bone broth, boxty with goat's cheese, pork knuckle roasted in stout and slow-cooked beef with champ. The meat-free menu is usually based on pulses or mushrooms.

Allergens are marked by number on every dish, following the EU list of 14. The regular menu includes 1 cereals containing gluten, 3 eggs, 7 milk, 9 celery and 12 sulphites. The list for the current menu hangs at the dining room entrance, and we'll bring it to your table on request.

07A year at the guesthouse

Through the seasons

We're open all year apart from two weeks' maintenance in November. We publish the exact dates in September and let guests with bookings know in advance.

Dec – Mar

Winter

Crisp, clear days bring the best views from the ridge. We drop walkers at the Kerry Way 4 km away at 8:30 in the morning and collect them at 16:00.

  • The drying room runs all day
  • Christmas and the February mid-term, 3 nights minimum
  • New Year's Eve, 4 nights minimum
  • The lane can be icy early in the morning

Apr – May

Spring

Paths above the treeline can be boggy in places until mid-May, while the valley routes are dry. It's our quietest time of year, and rooms are often free even a week ahead.

  • The waterfall in the ravine is at its fullest
  • Dinner Thursday to Sunday
  • Sauna daily 16:00 – 21:00
  • Free dates even a week ahead

Jun – Aug

Summer

Breakfast is served on the terrace and the fire pit is lit every evening. The last two weeks of August are the busiest.

  • Book 4 – 6 weeks ahead
  • Book the gazebo by 18:00
  • Cots free of charge
  • Ridge walk, setting off before 7:00

Sep – Nov

Autumn

The red deer rut echoes around the valleys, and mornings on the ridge are often frosty. We light the stove in the lounge from mid-September.

  • Red deer rut in October
  • Two weeks' maintenance in November
  • Stays of 3 nights or more are common
  • It gets dark early, so bring a head torch

08Reviews

What our guests say

A week after they leave, we text our guests one question. We publish the answers with a first name and the month of the stay, and only ever shorten them.

We don't edit reviews or pick only the good ones. If something about your stay isn't right, tell reception while you're still here — that's when we can fix it.

09Process

How booking works

Four steps from enquiry to handing over the key. None of them needs payment in advance — we don't take a deposit for individual rooms.

01

Enquiry

Fill in the booking form or call reception. We need your dates, the number of guests and the room type.

The form shows a guide price for your stay straight away, dinners included.

02

Confirmation within 24 hours

We'll get back to you by phone or text, confirming the dates and the exact price for the season, or offering the nearest available dates.

Your booking only stands once we've confirmed it.

03

Two days before arrival

We'll remind you of your dates and agree an arrival time. That's when to tell us about dinners, a cot, a dog or a lift from the bus stop.

Changing the number of guests is free up to this point.

04

Arrival and payment

Check-in is from 14:00, and you collect your key at reception. If you arrive late, we'll leave it in the key safe by the entrance and text you the code.

You pay when you leave, by card or in cash; businesses get an invoice.

If the dates you want are taken, we'll offer the nearest available ones and put you on the waiting list. If there's a cancellation, you'll be the first we contact.

11House rules

Terms, cancellations and payments

We keep our rules short and the same for everyone. Anything outside them can be arranged by phone in advance.

Booking and confirmation
An enquiry through the form doesn't commit you to anything. We confirm the dates and price within 24 hours by phone or text, and the booking only stands after that.
Deposit
We don't take a deposit for individual rooms. For groups of five rooms or more, the deposit is 30% of the accommodation price.
Cancelling a stay
Free of charge up to 7 days before arrival. From 7 to 2 days before, 50% of the first night's price; less than 48 hours before, 100% of the first night's price.
Group cancellations
Free of charge up to 21 days before arrival; after that, the deposit paid is forfeited. Changing the number of rooms up to 7 days before is free.
Payment methods
Cash, card at reception, or bank transfer on invoice for businesses. We don't accept meal vouchers or employee benefit cards.
VAT
Included in all our prices. There are no other taxes or charges to pay on arrival.
Check-in and check-out
Check-in from 14:00, check-out by 10:00. If you're arriving after 20:00, please let us know in advance and we'll arrange how you get your key.
Quiet hours
From 22:00 to 7:00. The lounge is open until 22:00; after that, please move to the fire pit or your apartment.
Children and extra beds
Children under 6 stay free in their parents' room. An extra bed is €18 a night, and a cot is free if you let us know.
Dogs
€10 a night, by arrangement in advance, with no more than two dogs in an apartment. Dogs aren't allowed in the dining room or the sauna; a blanket is available at reception.
Damage and lost keys
Any damage is settled on site at the actual cost of repair. A lost key costs €20 to replace the lock cylinder.
Smoking
Smoking isn't allowed anywhere in the house, balconies included. You can smoke under the shelter by the main entrance.

12FAQ

Answers to common questions

If your answer isn't here, call reception on your number here or write to us using the form below. We reply the same day, and by the evening at weekends.

Is breakfast really included in the price?

Yes. The room price includes the breakfast buffet from 7:30 to 10:00 for everyone staying, children included. If you're setting off early, we'll make you a packed breakfast, also at no extra charge. Just tell reception by 20:00 the day before.

Can we bring a dog?

Yes, by arrangement in advance. The charge is €10 per dog per night, with no more than two dogs in an apartment. We lend a blanket and a bowl at reception.

Dogs aren't allowed in the dining room, the sauna or the lounge. Please keep dogs on a lead in shared areas; around the house they can roam freely and nobody minds.

Where can we park, and is there a charge?

There are eight free spaces right by the house, no booking needed. In winter the car park is gritted by 7:00.

When the house is full, there's more parking 100 metres down the lane at the turn-off, also free. Please let us know in advance if you're bringing a trailer or a van.

Can we get to you without a car?

Yes. A Local Link bus runs from the railway station up the valley, and the stop is a 10-minute walk from the guesthouse. The journey from the station takes about 35 minutes.

If you tell us your arrival time in advance, we'll collect you from the bus stop in our van at no charge. The same goes for your departure.

Is the guesthouse wheelchair accessible?

Partly. Room 01 is on the ground floor, reached through the side entrance with no step; its doors are 90 cm wide and the shower is level-access. Reception, the dining room and the lounge are also on the ground floor with no steps.

The sauna is in the basement, down 12 steps, so it isn't accessible. The other rooms are on the first floor and in the attic, and there's no lift.

How does the sauna work?

The sauna is included in your stay and is heated daily from 16:00 to 21:00. It takes six people, so hours are booked at reception, usually in the morning at breakfast.

Towels are in your room, and you'll get a sheet to sit on at the sauna. Children under 12 only with an adult.

Do we have to pay a deposit in advance?

Not for individual rooms — you pay on site by card or in cash. We only ask for a deposit from groups of five rooms or more, and it's 30% of the accommodation price.

We invoice businesses on 14-day terms; we need your billing details before you arrive.

What if we arrive late in the evening?

Reception is open until 20:00. Arriving later is no problem — just let us know in your booking or by phone on the day.

We'll leave your key in a key safe by the entrance and text you the code. There's no charge for this.

  • 1 By car

    Turn off the main road at the bridge in Glenridge and follow the signs for the Kerry Way. The last 2 km climb on a tarmac lane that's gritted by 7:00 in winter.
