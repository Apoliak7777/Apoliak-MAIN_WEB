FAQ

What you ask us most often

Is my old laptop still worth repairing?

We'll tell you honestly, even when we won't make money on it. Our rule is simple: when a repair costs more than half the price of a comparable second-hand machine, we recommend replacing it. With laptops over eight years old, finding parts is often a problem too.

Will I lose my data?

Not with a normal repair. Before any work on a drive we make a copy, as long as the drive is readable — that's included, not an extra. When recovering data from a damaged drive, we always work from an image, never directly on the original.

Will you see my files?

The technician may see them while working — otherwise they couldn't check the data was transferred. We don't open them or copy them anywhere. If you have something sensitive on the computer, tell us and we'll do the repair so the drive isn't fitted into another machine.

Do you work on Apple too?

The everyday jobs, yes — drive and battery replacements, reinstalls and data recovery. We don't do motherboard repairs; we send those to a specialist repair shop and tell you where.

What does “remote support” mean?

We connect to your screen through a program you start yourself, and you can see everything we do the whole time. Close it and the connection ends. We solve most software problems like this within half an hour, with no call-out.

Do you have a loan computer?

With a support contract, yes — we lend you a laptop free of charge while yours is being repaired. For a one-off repair it's €4 a day, if one is available.
