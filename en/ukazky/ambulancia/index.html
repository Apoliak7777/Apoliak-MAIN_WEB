What the schemes cover

For medical card holders: an exam, fillings in the basic version, extractions and emergency treatment for pain. For PRSI patients: one exam per calendar year and a contribution to a cleaning.

What the patient pays

Premium materials, white composite fillings in full, dental hygiene, crowns and dentures, whitening and treatment beyond what the scheme covers. You'll find the amounts in the treatment price list.

If you don't have your card with you

We'll still treat you. You can give us your details at your next visit or over the phone. If your cover isn't confirmed, we charge the treatment as for a private patient.

12Terms

Payment, guarantee and cancellation

The rules people ask about most often on the phone. They're the same for new and registered patients and we don't change them during treatment.

Confirming your appointment

We confirm bookings by phone within one working day and send a text reminder the day before. The appointment only stands once we've confirmed it; sending the form doesn't create a booking.

Cancelling and moving an appointment

24 h

That's how much notice we need to offer the freed slot to another patient. You can cancel or move an appointment by mobile or landline, and we agree a new time during the call.

Payment methods

  • cash
  • card, contactless too
  • invoice payable within 14 days for companies with an agreement
  • practice gift voucher

You pay after treatment, not in advance. We only take a deposit for crowns, bridges and dentures.

Receipt

You get a receipt on the spot as soon as you pay. On request we issue it in your name and fill in a Med 2 form, so you can claim tax relief on non-routine dental treatment or claim from your insurer.

Late arrival and missed appointments

If you're more than 15 minutes late, we may not manage everything planned and will shorten the treatment. We don't charge for the first missed appointment. For the second and any further ones we charge €20 for the reserved time.

Guarantee on our work

Crowns, bridges and removable dentures come with a 24-month guarantee, fillings with 12 months. Under guarantee we repair or remake the work at no extra cost. The condition is a check-up every six months. The guarantee doesn't cover injuries or work by another dentist.

Deposit for crowns and dentures

Before we order work from the dental laboratory we take a 30% deposit. You pay the rest when the finished work is fitted. If you withdraw after the impression has gone to the laboratory, the deposit isn't refunded.

Call or send a message through the form. We prepare the voucher within two working days and you collect it at the practice. You pay on collection, by cash or card.

Using a voucher

14Practice

Premises and equipment

A small practice with two chairs. Nobody waits in the corridor and nobody treats two patients at once.

Two surgeries

One is for exams and restorative work, the other for dental hygiene. We have the X-ray right in the surgery, so you don't go anywhere else for it.

Waiting room

Seven seats, room for a buggy, drinking water and a coat rack. The children's corner is separate from the adult seating.

Who treats you

The same dentist handles the plan and the finished work; we don't swap during your treatment. Names and who does what are in the Who will treat you section.

Sterilisation and materials

We sterilise instruments in a class B autoclave and log every cycle. Single-use materials are used for one patient only. We choose materials for fillings, crowns and dentures according to the work and name them in the plan in advance.

Equipment in the surgery

Intraoral X-ray, an ultrasonic scaler, an air polisher and a curing light. You see your X-ray on the screen right from the chair.

If you're nervous about treatment

Tell us at the start. We adjust the pace, take breaks and explain every step beforehand. We can limit the first session to just the exam and the plan, with no treatment.

16FAQ

FAQ

If you don't find the answer here, write it in the form message or ask when you call. We reply to messages within one working day.

What should I bring to my first visit?

Your medical card or PRSI details, photo ID and a list of the medicines you take. If you have older X-rays or a report from your previous dentist, bring those too.

Are you taking new patients?

We confirm availability when you book. Send the form or call and we'll get back to you with the next free appointment. For dental hygiene and emergency treatment we also see patients who aren't registered with us.

What do the public schemes cover?

Some treatment is covered by the medical card scheme or PRSI Treatment Benefit. When you book we ask what applies to you, and for each treatment we tell you what you'll pay.

How does payment work?

After treatment, by cash or card. You get a receipt on the spot as soon as you pay; on request we issue it in your name with a Med 2 form. Companies with an agreement get an invoice payable within 14 days.

How do I cancel or move an appointment?

call us, or the landline on your number here, at least 24 hours in advance. We agree a new appointment during the call. If you can't get through, send a message through the form and we'll get back to you within one working day.

What if I don't have my medical card with me?

We'll still treat you. You can give us your details at your next visit or over the phone. If your cover isn't confirmed, we charge the treatment as for a private patient.

Can I get a gift voucher?

Yes. We prepare a voucher for dental hygiene, whitening or an amount of your choice from €30 within two working days, and you collect it at the practice. It's valid for 12 months and can also cover part of a treatment.

Do you use anaesthetic — will it hurt?

For treatments that need it, we give a local anaesthetic. We numb the injection site first with a gel. If you're nervous, tell us at the start and we'll adapt the pace and the length of the session.

From what age do you treat children?

We recommend the first check-up at around age three, or any time earlier if there are problems. The first visit is usually just getting to know the surgery. A parent stays in the room during treatment.

What should I do if my tooth hurts today?

Call first thing in the morning — we keep a short emergency slot every day we're open. With facial swelling, a temperature or an injured tooth, call straight away and say so at the start of the call.
