03 How it works

How selling works, in six steps

From the first visit to closing. For an apartment with a clean title, the whole process usually takes 9 to 14 weeks; for a house with a right of way or a probate sale, longer.

  1. 01within 48 hours

    Visit and valuation

    We walk through the property, measure it and check the folio. The visit and a verbal valuation are free. A written valuation comparing five recent sales from the Property Price Register costs €120, and it's deducted from our fee if you sell through us.

  2. 02Week 1

    Agreement and paperwork

    We sign a letter of engagement setting out the asking price, our fee and the term. We arrange the BER certificate and floor plan, and fill in any missing documents.

  3. 03within 3 days

    Preparation and marketing

    We photograph the inside and outside, draw a floor plan with room sizes, and list the property on six portals, on this website and in our buyer database.

  4. 04ongoing

    Viewings and feedback

    We handle the viewings, so you don't need to be there. Every other week you get a written report on how often the listing was opened, the viewings and buyers' comments.

  5. 057 to 30 days

    Sale agreed and finance

    Once a sale is agreed, the buyer pays a booking deposit into our client account. If the buyer is taking out a mortgage, we coordinate with the lender and the valuer.

  6. 066 – 8 weeks to closing

    Contracts and closing

    The solicitors draw up and exchange contracts, and we prepare the handover with meter readings. On closing, the money is released, we hand over the keys and the utilities are transferred.

07 Financing your purchase

Mortgages and the approval timeline

Work out a guide repayment in the calculator in the panel above. Here you'll find what the lender asks for, how long each step takes and what we handle with you.

What the lender asks the buyer for

  • ID and a salary certificate
  • Your latest tax return and accounts if you're self-employed
  • Bank statements, usually for the last six months
  • The folio and a valuation report on the property
  • Confirmation of the agreed price (sales advice note)
  • A deposit of at least 10% of the price — Central Bank rules cap borrowing at 90% of the value

We gather the paperwork with you so the lender doesn't send the application back for more.

08 Lettings

Lettings and property management

We can find you a tenant as a one-off, or manage the property for the whole tenancy. The landlord pays our letting fee; the tenant pays nothing.

Letting service

  • A fee of one month's rent, minimum €450
  • Tenant checks: ID, employment and references
  • A tenancy agreement, with rent reviews within Rent Pressure Zone rules
  • An inventory with meter readings and photos
  • A deposit of one month's rent, the legal maximum
  • Registering the tenancy with the RTB

If the tenant leaves within three months, we find a new one at no extra fee.

Management during the tenancy

  • A monthly fee of 8% of the rent, minimum €35 a month
  • Tracking payments and chasing arrears
  • Repairs through trusted tradespeople
  • Inspections twice a year, with a written report
  • A yearly statement of rent and costs for your tax return
  • Attending the management company AGM, by arrangement

You can end the management agreement with two months' notice, with no penalty.

12 Terms

Terms of business, guarantees and cancellations

You get the draft letter of engagement in print at least a day before signing, and it's signed at the next meeting. Agreements don't renew automatically — they end when their term is up.

What we guarantee in writing

  • The fee in the agreement doesn't change, even if the sale takes longer
  • We never reduce the asking price without your written consent
  • You get a written report on viewings and interest every other week
  • If the listing isn't live within 3 working days of signing, the fee drops by 0.4 percentage points
  • If there isn't a single viewing within 60 days of going live, you can end the agreement immediately, without the 30-day notice
  • We're fully liable for any error in paperwork we've prepared

What we don't guarantee

  • We can't guarantee the price the property finally sells for
  • We have no say in the lender's decision on the buyer's mortgage
  • The solicitors' timeline isn't in our hands — from sale agreed to closing usually takes 8 to 12 weeks
  • We don't promise a sale date — for an apartment with a clean title it usually takes 9 to 14 weeks
  • We don't give tax advice — capital gains tax on a sale is a matter for your accountant

Letter of engagement

  • Sole agency for 6 months, with no automatic renewal
  • 30 days' notice, without giving a reason
  • You set the asking price; we give our recommendation
  • Change the price at any time, in writing
  • Our fee is only due when contracts are signed

Booking deposit

  • A booking deposit of €3,000, held in our client account
  • The property is taken off the market for 30 days, or 45 with a mortgage
  • The deposit counts towards the purchase price
  • If the lender doesn't approve the mortgage, it's refunded in full
  • Until contracts are signed, it's refundable in any case, as the rules require

Cancelling a viewing or meeting

  • Cancelling a viewing costs nothing — just call
  • Please let us know at least 2 hours before
  • We'll arrange a new time by phone, usually within 48 hours
  • We can also move a viewing to a Saturday
  • You can cancel a separately booked valuation up to 24 hours before, free of charge

13 FAQ

Answers to common questions

If your question isn't here, write to us through the contact form or give us a call. We reply within 24 hours on working days.

When do I pay your fee, and how much?

The seller pays the fee, and it's only due when contracts are signed, within 7 days. With sole agency it's 2.4% of the sale price, minimum €1,900. If the property doesn't sell, you pay nothing — not for advertising, viewings or the BER certificate.

Do I have to sign a sole agency agreement?

No. Without sole agency the fee is 3.2% and the agreement runs for 3 months. Sole agency lets us invest more in preparation and marketing, so the rate is lower. You can end it with 30 days' notice without giving a reason.

How do you value my property?

We start from actual sales of comparable properties in the same area over the last 12 months, from the Property Price Register — not from asking prices. We factor in the condition, floor, aspect, BER and running costs. You get a written range and a recommended price; the final decision is yours.

How long does a sale take?

For an apartment with a clean title and complete paperwork, usually 9 to 14 weeks from signing with us to closing. From sale agreed, the solicitors usually need 8 to 12 weeks. Probate, a right of way or an extension without planning make it longer, and we'll warn you about that at the start.

Do I have to be there for viewings?

No. Once we have the keys, we handle viewings ourselves, evenings and weekends too. We always check in advance that the buyer has real financing in place. After every viewing you get a short report on what the buyer liked and what put them off.

What if the buyer's mortgage isn't approved?

That's covered up front. With a mortgage the property is reserved for 45 days, and if the lender doesn't approve the loan, the booking deposit is refunded to the buyer in full. The property goes back on the market and we carry on with other buyers.

I'm selling an apartment with a mortgage on it. Is that possible?

Yes, it's common. Your solicitor gets a redemption figure from the lender, and on closing the price is split: part goes straight to clearing the mortgage and releasing the charge, and the rest to you. The solicitors handle the whole process, so neither side carries any risk.

Do you handle lettings too?

Yes. The fee is one month's rent, minimum €450, paid by the landlord. It includes tenant checks, the tenancy agreement, the inventory with meter readings and RTB registration. We can also manage the letting for the whole tenancy.

We're selling an inherited property — what do we need?

You'll need a grant of probate, or letters of administration, for the estate. Until it issues, we can prepare and market the property, but contracts can only be signed once the grant is in place. The personal representative named in the grant signs on behalf of the estate.
