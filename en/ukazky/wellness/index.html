About the studio

Five rules we stick to

The studio has five treatment rooms, separate changing rooms and an upstairs sauna area with a relaxation room. We let no more than 14 people into the sauna area at a time.

  1. Fifteen minutes between bookings

    The time shown is time on the table, not including changing. That's why appointments don't run late and nobody waits in the corridor.

  2. A consultation form on your first visit

    One page: injuries, operations, pregnancy, allergies to oils and medication. So on your first visit, please arrive twenty minutes early.

  3. You set the pressure

    We agree the pressure at the start and can change it at any time during the massage. Just say — the therapist won't ask every five minutes.

  4. Quiet in the sauna area and the relaxation room

    Phones stay in the lockers in the changing room. Calls and photos only at reception.

  5. No selling after your treatment

    We won't try to sell you creams or courses. If you ask, we'll advise you; we won't bring it up ourselves.

Sauna etiquette

Rules for the sauna area

The rules apply to everyone equally, and staff make sure they're followed. If they're broken repeatedly, we'll end your session without a refund.

  1. Shower before every round

    Always shower and dry off before going into the sauna or steam room, and always wear swimwear.

  2. A towel under your whole body

    Always put a large towel on the bench, under your feet too. You'll get one when you arrive.

  3. No jewellery or watches

    Metal gets very hot in the sauna and can burn. Leave jewellery, watches and glasses in your locker.

  4. Only staff pour water on the stones

    Don't pour water on the stones yourself. Don't go in or out during an infusion.

  5. Rinse before the plunge

    Always rinse off before the cold plunge. Keep your head above the water; no jumping.

  6. Quiet and phones

    Keep your voice down in the sauna area and the relaxation room. Calls and photos only at reception.

When not to use the sauna

  • With a temperature, an acute infection or an open wound.
  • When pregnant, or after drinking alcohol.
  • On an empty stomach, or straight after a hard workout.
  • With a heart condition, unstable blood pressure or after recent surgery, check with your doctor first.
  • Children under 15 aren't allowed in the sauna area.

Your visit

Five steps from booking to leaving

On your first visit, please arrive twenty minutes early for the consultation form; after that, ten minutes is enough.

  1. Booking

    Through the form below or by phone. We confirm within two hours during opening hours.

  2. Arrival

    Arrive ten minutes before your appointment. At reception you'll get a locker key, a robe and a towel.

  3. A quick chat

    The therapist asks about sore spots, injuries and the pressure you'd like. It takes about three minutes.

  4. Treatment

    The time shown is time on the table. You can change the pressure at any point during the massage.

  5. Rest

    After your massage we recommend fifteen minutes in the relaxation room. Water and herbal tea are unlimited.

Practical tips

  • Have your last big meal at least two hours before your treatment.
  • After your massage, drink at least an extra litre of water during the day.
  • After a deep tissue massage, muscles can be tender for two days — that's normal.
  • If anything feels uncomfortable, say so straight away, not at the end.

Preparation

What to bring, and what we provide

You don't need to bring anything for a massage — the robe and towel are included. For the sauna area you need swimwear, or you can hire it for €4.

Bring

  • Swimwear for the sauna area — if you forget it, you can hire some for €4.
  • A hair tie — handy for long hair in the sauna and during a massage.
  • A list of medication — especially blood thinners and blood pressure medication; the name is enough.
  • Parent or guardian's consent — for visitors aged 15 to 18, on their first visit.
  • Your gift voucher number — if you'd like to use it and don't have it with you.

We provide

  • A robe, a large towel and slippers — included with every treatment and sauna session.
  • A locker with a key — in the changing room, for your phone, jewellery and watch.
  • Shower gel, shampoo and a hairdryer — in both changing rooms.
  • Water and herbal tea — unlimited in the relaxation room.
  • The oil of your choice — neutral, or one of three aromatherapy blends.

What to wear

You keep your underwear on during a massage, and you're covered with a sheet the whole time; only the part being worked on is uncovered. We don't recommend contact lenses in the steam room. After an oil massage, dark clothes are a better idea, as oil soaks into light fabrics.

What to leave at home

Please don't bring food in; the relaxation room is for drinks only. Drinks in glass bottles don't belong in the sauna area. We recommend leaving valuables at home — the lockers lock, but we can't be responsible for what's in them.

FAQ

What people ask most often

If your answer isn't here, call reception or use the form at the bottom of the page.

Do I need to book, or can I just turn up?

You need to book for a massage — a free table without a booking is rare. You can come to the sauna area without booking, but it holds 14 people and is full on Saturday afternoons. If you're only coming for the sauna, call half an hour before to check there's space.

What should I bring, and what do I wear for a massage?

You don't need anything for a massage. The robe, large towel and slippers are included; you keep your underwear on, and you're covered during the massage — only the part being worked on is uncovered. For the sauna area you need swimwear, or you can hire it for €4.

Can I use the sauna without a massage?

Yes. A two-hour session on its own costs €16, and three hours €21. The area is shared by women and men, and swimwear is worn. Last entry is 90 minutes before closing.

Is massage suitable during pregnancy?

We offer pregnancy massage from the thirteenth week, lying on your side with supports. We don't massage in the first trimester. If there are complications, we'll ask for your doctor's approval. We don't recommend the sauna during pregnancy.

How early should I arrive?

Ten minutes is enough. On your first visit, allow twenty minutes for the consultation form. The treatment time shown is time on the table, so arriving late shortens your massage if the next slot is booked.

What's the minimum age?

Massage and the sauna area are for ages 15 and up. Under 18, we need a signed consent from a parent or guardian, brought on the first visit. We don't massage younger children.

Can I use a gift voucher for a pass?

No — vouchers can be used for massages, sauna sessions and extras, but not to buy a pass or the wellness card. Any unused value stays on the voucher for its twelve-month validity and can be used on your next visit.

Can two of us come and be in the same room?

Yes — we have a double room, and couples massage is done by two therapists at the same time: 60 minutes for €96 and 90 minutes for €132, for both of you. For this booking we take a 30% deposit, as it ties up two therapists at once.

How do I cancel or move an appointment?

Call reception on your number here or write to us using the form at the bottom of the page. Up to 24 hours before, cancelling or moving is free; after that, the cancellation terms above apply.
