How it works

Five steps from enquiry to signature

You write or call

Just tell us from where, to where, what and when. For removals, a few photos help too — they tell us the volume better than the number of rooms.

We send a quote

Within two hours during working hours. We quote one price, not a “from” price — and it already includes fuel and tolls.

You confirm the date

We agree a loading time and send you the driver's name and a consignment number so you can track the job.

We deliver

The driver calls before arriving to load and before unloading. If they're running late, you'll hear it from us before you have to ask.

We hand over and invoice

You sign the delivery note on site, and the invoice comes by email, payable within 14 days. For businesses, 30 days is possible too.

FAQ

What you ask us most often

How is the load insured?

Every run is covered by the carrier's statutory liability insurance, and international runs by CMR insurance. But that cover has a limit based on weight, not on the value of the goods. If you're sending something valuable and light — electronics, instruments, art — take out extra cover for its real value. It costs around 1.5% of the value and we'll arrange it for you.

How long does it take to move a two-bed apartment?

With two porters and a lift, usually five to seven hours, including the drive. Without a lift, allow two hours more. If you pack yourself and the furniture is already dismantled, it goes much faster — and costs less, because we charge porters by the hour.

Can you come at weekends or at night?

Yes — we move offices almost exclusively at weekends. The weekend surcharge is 20%, and a night run between 22:00 and 6:00 is also 20%. They don't add up — a weekend night is still 20%.

What won't you carry?

Dangerous goods under ADR, live animals, chilled food, and cash or valuables. We do take pianos, safes and aquariums, but we need to know in advance — they need a different vehicle and a different number of people.

Do I pay in advance?

Private customers pay after unloading, by card or in cash. We invoice businesses with 14-day terms, or 30 days for regular customers. We only ask for a deposit on international runs over €1,500.

What if something gets damaged?

It goes on the delivery note straight away on site — that's important, because a claim made later is much harder. We settle damage within 30 days. In eighteen years we've had twenty-seven insurance claims and paid out on all of them, even where the insurer paid less.
