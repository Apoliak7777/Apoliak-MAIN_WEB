Seasonal changes to the menu

From April to October we have fresh herb lemonades and iced coffee, and in winter mulled wine and thick hot chocolate. We top the salad and the cheesecake with fruit in season, so the ingredients change through the year.

We change our coffee beans every two months. The current blend is written on the board by the bar, and we sell it in bags for home too.

Diets and allergens

We make the gluten-free base on a separate board for an extra €1.50, but the kitchen isn't gluten-free and we can't guarantee there are no traces of flour.

Vegan dishes are named on the menu: the vegetable pizza with no cheese, porridge made with water and the roast courgette salad without ricotta. We have oat and almond plant milk for €0.40.

04About us

The café, the space and the kitchen

A café with 42 seats and a courtyard terrace for 24. The dough proves for 48 hours in the cold, and we bake the pizza in a stone oven at 430 degrees.

In the morning we're a café; from eleven we serve the lunch menu, and in the afternoon and evening stone-oven pizza. The kitchen takes last orders 45 minutes before the café closes.

  • Seating and terrace

    42 seats inside, 24 on the courtyard terrace. The terrace is open from April to October.

  • Takeaway orders

    By phone or through the form. Usually ready within 15 minutes, within 25 at lunchtime and on Friday evenings.

  • Larger groups

    Please book at least a day ahead for groups of more than 8. For business lunches we'll agree a fixed time and menu.

  • Step-free access

    The entrance from Linden Street is step-free, and a ramp from the courtyard leads to the terrace.

The space and who works in it

The main room has tall windows onto the street and a long oak table for ten that can be joined or split. By the window there are four tables for two with sockets, and at the back a quieter area with soft benches. The bar is right by the entrance, so you can collect a takeaway without walking through the whole room.

The courtyard terrace is separated from the street by a passageway, and no cars come into the courtyard. There are 90 centimetres between the tables, enough for a wheelchair or a buggy.

  • Barista

    In the morning they're on the lever machine, making today's filter batch and looking after the grind and the coffee setup.

  • Pizza chef

    Stretches the dough, runs the stone oven and keeps up the pace during the evening rush.

  • Kitchen – prep

    Prepares the bases, vegetables and soups for the lunch menu, and breakfast in the morning.

  • Floor staff

    Split into two sections, inside and on the terrace. At lunchtime there's an extra person handing out takeaway orders.

06The day

Our daily rhythm

The café, lunches and the evening oven take turns at fixed times. Use them to plan your visit or when to collect an order.

  1. 7:30

    We open

    The first batch of filter coffee, the lever machine set up for the day's blend. The morning pastries are on the counter until they sell out.

  2. 8:00

    Full breakfast

    Scrambled eggs, sourdough bread, yoghurt with granola and porridge. The breakfast menu is served until 11:00, and until 12:00 at weekends.

  3. 11:00

    Lunch menu begins

    Soup, main and a drink for €8.90. We recommend ordering takeaway by 10:30 so you don't have to wait.

  4. 14:30

    End of lunch

    The big tables free up and we go back to being a café. Soup and salads are still served while they last.

  5. 17:00

    The oven is in full swing

    The stone oven holds 430 degrees and a pizza is done in 90 seconds. We recommend booking on Friday and Saturday.

  6. 21:15

    Last kitchen orders

    On Friday and Saturday until 22:15. After that the café serves only drinks and desserts until closing.

07Getting here

Getting here

We're on the corner of Linden Street and Orchard Street, and the entrance is straight from Linden Street. The terrace is in the courtyard behind the building, through the passageway next to the entrance.

Open 8 Linden Street on the map

  • By bus

    The nearest stop is 30 metres from the entrance on Linden Street, on the same side, so you don't need to cross the road. From the stop, walk towards the corner of Orchard Street.

  • By car and parking

    You can't stop outside, and the nearest spaces are on Orchard Street. They're pay and display on weekdays from 8:00 to 18:00; parking is free at weekends and in the evening. You can stop briefly in the courtyard passageway to collect an order.

  • By bike

    There's a stand for six bikes by the entrance. We can keep a scooter or a folding bike in the passageway — just please don't leave them in the aisle between the tables.

  • Step-free access

    The entrance from the street is step-free, and the doorway is 90 cm wide. A ramp leads from the courtyard to the terrace. The accessible toilet is on the ground floor just past the bar.

  • With children

    Two high chairs and a baby-changing counter in the toilet. There's a spot by the window where you can park a buggy without blocking the aisle.

09Vouchers and larger events

Vouchers, business lunches and private events

You can buy a voucher at the bar during opening hours. We arrange business lunches and private events through the form, so you have the terms in writing.

  • Gift voucher

    Worth 20, 30 or €50. Valid for 12 months from issue on everything we sell, including coffee beans to take home. We issue them with or without a name, on the spot. Vouchers can't be exchanged for cash; any remaining value stays on the voucher until it's used up.

  • Business lunches

    We'll agree a fixed time of 11:30 or 12:30, a menu for the whole week in advance, and one monthly invoice instead of paying one by one. Suits four or more people a day.

  • Group bookings of 9 or more

    You choose from three set menus so the kitchen can serve everyone at once. We confirm the booking by phone the day before, and we can split the bill per person if agreed in advance.

  • Private event using the whole space

    We rent out the whole venue on Sundays from 18:00 and on Mondays. There are 42 seats inside and 24 on the terrace. We'll agree the menu, time and staffing; the deposit is 30% of the agreed amount.

11FAQ

Frequently asked questions

If you can't find the answer here, write to us through the form or give us a call.

Do I need to book a table?

On weekday mornings, usually not. On Friday and Saturday after 18:00 and at lunchtime between 12:00 and 13:00 we recommend booking; otherwise you may wait 15 to 20 minutes.

Do you have gluten-free pizza?

Yes — the gluten-free base is €1.50 extra and we stretch it on a separate board. But the kitchen isn't gluten-free and we can't rule out traces of flour in the air, so always tell the staff if you have a severe allergy.

Do you have anything for vegans?

The vegetable pizza with no cheese is vegan, as are the roast courgette salad without ricotta and porridge made with water. For coffee we have oat and almond milk for an extra €0.40.

Can I pay by card?

Yes — card, cash or contactless by phone. We can split the bill; for groups of more than 8, please arrange that before ordering.

Can I bring my dog?

On the terrace, yes, no restrictions. Inside outside lunchtime by arrangement with the staff, as long as the dog stays by the table. We'll bring water for your dog if you ask.

Do you deliver?

No, we don't deliver. We're collection only: we usually have orders ready within 15 minutes, or 25 at busy times. We recommend eating a boxed pizza within half an hour.

Can I work on a laptop here?

Yes, Wi-Fi is free and there are sockets at the long table and by the window. Between 11:00 and 14:30 please free up the big tables for lunch; the back area is quieter then.

Can I hire the whole space?

Yes, on Sundays from 18:00 and all day Monday. We'll agree the menu, time and staffing; the deposit is 30% of the agreed amount. Write to us through the form and choose Private event as the type of request.

12Terms

Bookings, cancellations and payment

The rules are the same for individuals and groups; only the cancellation deadlines differ.

Booking

We hold your table for 20 minutes from the agreed time. If you're running late, give us a call and we'll hold it longer if we can.

We confirm bookings by phone and reply to form messages within 24 hours. A booking isn't valid until we've confirmed it.

Cancellations

You can cancel an ordinary booking any time free of charge. For groups of more than 8, please cancel at least 24 hours in advance.

For a private event the deposit is fully refundable up to 7 days before the date; after that we use it for ingredients already bought.

Payment

Card, cash or contactless by phone.

Service is included in the price; tips are voluntary. We invoice business customers monthly with 14-day terms.

Takeaway orders

We have your order ready at the agreed time. If you don't collect it within 30 minutes, we can no longer guarantee it'll be hot.

Packaging costs €0.30. We're happy to fill your own container if it's clean and big enough.

Allergens and ingredients

Allergen numbers are shown for every item on the menu and for every day of the lunch menu; you'll find the list below the menu. The staff will also talk you through what's in a dish.

Our ingredients change with the seasons, so the fruit in a dessert or the vegetables in a salad may vary.

On the premises

No smoking inside; smoking is allowed on the terrace at the back by the wall.

Please don't bring your own food and drink, except baby food, or a cake for a celebration if agreed in advance.
