Why the rows don't add up

The phases partly overlap — while the roofer is working on the roof, the services are already going in inside. So the total row shows the real duration, including the overlap and a margin for weather.

Seasonal limits

We pour concrete down to −3 °C with an admixture, and stop below that. We apply render between +5 and +30 °C, and never in direct sun.

Drying and curing times

Cement screed dries at about 1 cm a week, anhydrite faster. We only lay floors after measuring the residual moisture, even if it means a week's delay.

When we can start

We usually have capacity 6 to 10 weeks ahead. Smaller jobs of up to 3 weeks can sometimes start sooner, if a crew is free between two stages.

How the timescales work

We count the completion date in the contract in working days, and it already includes a margin for normal weather. If we delay the job through our own fault, we pay a penalty of 0.05% of the contract price for every day of delay.

What extends the timescale

Frost below −5 °C for wet trades, changes to the brief during the build, waiting for a planning decision, or waiting for materials you chose to supply yourself. We record every such delay in the site diary.

Reporting a defect under guarantee

Report a defect by phone on your number here or through the form on this page. We always acknowledge it within 48 hours, and inspect within the time shown for that type of work in the table. We fix ordinary defects within 15 working days, and come straight away for anything that stops you using the building.

08 — Instalments

Payments tied to stages

You don't pay for the whole build up front. Each instalment is tied to a completed stage, which you sign off in the site diary.

  1. Materials deposit Due when you sign the contract. We use it to buy the materials for the first stage. 15%
  2. After the shell or strip-out Once the first stage is signed off in the site diary. 25%
  3. After the services and the roof When the building is weathertight and the services are ready for testing. 25%
  4. After the finishing work After plastering, floors and fitting the sanitaryware. 25%
  5. Retention Due 30 days after handover, once every recorded snag has been fixed. 10%

Payment methods

By bank transfer against an invoice with 14-day terms; we issue a deposit invoice when you sign the contract. We accept cash payments up to €300, always with a receipt. We don't offer card payments or finance.

What the price includes

Crew labour, site overheads, travel within 60 km, site facilities and a clean-up when we finish. Materials are a separate line in the estimate, with the unit price shown.

Insurance

We hold public liability insurance for our work. The policy number is in the building contract.

Variations during the build

Every change from the estimate has its own variation order, with the price and the effect on the timescale. We don't start anything or invoice anything without your signature on the variation order.

Omitted work

If you drop an item from the estimate, we deduct it in full, including materials. Omissions go on the same variation order as additions.

Hidden defects found during strip-out

A rotten joist, a cracked lintel or aluminium wiring can't be seen in advance. We stop work, record what we've found and propose a solution with a price. We only carry on once you agree.

Withdrawal on our side

We can only withdraw if the information provided was untrue, if we're kept off the site for more than 30 days, or if a payment is more than 30 days late. In that case we refund the deposit within 14 days.

14 — Questions

FAQ

The questions we're asked most often. If yours isn't here, put it in the form.

Is the calculator price final?

No, it's a guide. It's there so you know whether your plans fit your budget at all. The final price comes from the itemised estimate after the site visit, with the actual quantities, materials and site conditions. The difference between the calculator and the estimate is usually under 15%.

How do I send an enquiry when there's no email address on the site?

Use the form in the Contact section. Your message reaches the company inbox just like an email, and we reply within 24 hours. If it's urgent, call us during office hours.

Do you take on smaller jobs, not just whole houses?

Yes. The smallest job we take on is about €3,000 excluding VAT — a bathroom renovation or new floors in an apartment, for example. For smaller work we charge the hourly rate from the price list.

Do I need drawings before the site visit?

No. For renovations, it's enough for us to come and measure what's there. For a new build we need drawings, but we can arrange them through the architectural designer we work with, including planning permission.

Do you buy the materials, or do I?

Normally we supply the materials, and you'll see each one named and priced in the estimate. If you'd like to buy some items yourself — typically tiles, sanitaryware or flooring — that's fine, but they need to be on site before that stage starts, or the date moves.

Can I live in the apartment during the renovation?

For a complete renovation we don't recommend it — for the first three weeks the apartment has no water and there's dusty demolition work. For a partial renovation of one room or the bathroom you can stay; we seal the area off with a dust screen and only turn the water off at agreed times.

How do you deal with dust, noise and neighbours in an apartment block?

We only do noisy work on weekdays between 8:00 and 17:00, never at weekends. We cover the common areas with sheeting and clean the corridor every day. Before work starts, we put up a notice for the neighbours with the dates and the site manager's contact details.

What if something unexpected turns up during the strip-out?

A rotten joist, for example, a cracked lintel or aluminium wiring. We stop work at that spot, record what we've found, propose a solution and put the price on a variation order. We only carry on once you've signed it, so the invoice holds no surprises.

Can a site visit be arranged outside working hours?

We do site visits from 7:00 to 18:00, including early evenings. On Saturdays we'll come by arrangement, if nothing else works. Sunday is our day off, and our sites don't run.

How far from Galway do you go?

Usually up to 60 km, which covers Galway, Athenry, Oughterard, Loughrea, Oranmore and Tuam. We only go further for larger jobs over €60,000, and travel is then charged as per the price list.
