07 How it works

From order to handover

We tie most bouquets while you wait. For bigger arrangements we agree a date and confirm the mix in advance.

  1. 01

    Tell us the occasion and budget

    Just tell us who you want to make happy and for what occasion. If you can't decide, we'll advise you based on what's in the buckets that day.

  2. 02

    We pick flowers from the delivery

    We get cut flowers in three times a week. If something isn't the quality you asked for, we'll offer an alternative before we make up the arrangement.

  3. 03

    We tie it at the counter

    We tie an ordinary bouquet within 15 minutes and a bigger arrangement within an hour. Wedding and funeral flowers are made for an agreed date.

  4. 04

    We hand over or deliver

    The bouquet goes out wrapped, with water in the wrapping. For deliveries, the driver calls the recipient beforehand and we confirm the handover to the person who ordered.

13 Care

How to make flowers last longer at home

With every bigger arrangement and every plant we include a care card. Here's what it says.

How long they last
FlowersVase lifeWhat helps
Tulips and daffodils5 to 7 dayscold water, just a few centimetres in the vase
Roses7 to 10 daysan angled cut and fresh water every other day
Chrysanthemums10 to 14 daysremove any leaves below the waterline
Dried arrangementsseveral yearsno water, out of direct sun and damp
House plantspermanentlywatering depends on the type, feed from spring to autumn

Cut flowers

  • Cut two centimetres off the stems at an angle, and again every time you change the water.
  • Change the water every other day, and rinse the vase when you do.
  • Keep the bouquet out of direct sun and away from radiators.
  • Don't leave it next to a fruit bowl — ripening fruit makes flowers wilt faster.

House plants

  • Leave the plant in one place for the first two weeks so it can settle in.
  • Water only when the top layer of compost is dry to the touch.
  • Feed every two weeks from spring to autumn; don't feed in winter.
  • We repot into a bigger pot in the shop, from €4.00.

Dried and long-lasting arrangements

  • They need no water; just dust them gently now and then.
  • Colours fade in direct sun; a shady spot is ideal.
  • Dried grasses go soft in a damp bathroom; the living room is a better place for them.
  • We can top up and repair a damaged arrangement in the shop.

When to let us know

  • If a bouquet arrives damaged or wilted, get in touch within 48 hours.
  • For a plant with pests, bring in a photo and we'll tell you what to do.
  • If allergies make choosing tricky, we'll make a bouquet with no pollen and no strong scent.
  • Advice is free even if you didn't buy the flowers from us.

14 FAQ

What you ask us most often

If you can't find the answer here, write to us using the form below or call during opening hours.

How long does a cut bouquet last?

Five to ten days, depending on the flowers. At home, cut about two centimetres off the stems at an angle, change the water every other day and trim the stems again each time.

Keep the bouquet out of direct sun, away from radiators and away from a fruit bowl: ripening fruit gives off a gas that makes flowers wilt quickly.

Can I order delivery for a specific time?

For city delivery you can choose a two-hour window, for example between 14:00 and 16:00. We confirm a more precise time by phone on the morning of delivery.

For delivery at an exact time — before a ceremony or a party, say — we can arrange it by phone for orders over €60.

What happens if the recipient isn't in?

The driver calls them on the number you give when ordering. If there's no answer, the bouquet comes back to the shop and goes into water, and we contact you the same day.

A second delivery attempt is free, or you can collect from the shop. We'll keep the bouquet fresh for two days.

Will you write a card to go with the bouquet?

Yes, at no extra charge. Put your message in the note on your order; about 200 characters fit. We write it by hand on card in one of three colours.

If you'd like to stay anonymous, we'll sign the card however you wish, and we never put a receipt with the price in the delivery.

Do you do funeral flowers and deliver them to the service?

We make wreaths, arrangements and sympathy bouquets. We can make an ordinary arrangement within 24 hours; a 60 cm wreath from €45, an arrangement from €35.

On request we deliver straight to the funeral home or the cemetery at the agreed time. We can supply a ribbon with a printed message by the next day.

Do you have flowers with no strong scent and no pollen?

Yes. For a hospital or an office we make bouquets without strongly scented flowers — for example roses, chrysanthemums or lisianthus.

We remove the anthers from lilies in the shop so the pollen doesn't stain clothes or irritate allergies. Just mention it in the note on your order.

How should I look after a house plant after buying it?

With every plant we include a card about watering, light and feeding. Leave the plant in one place for the first two weeks so it can settle in.

If you're not sure, bring a photo into the shop or write to us through the contact form. We repot into a bigger pot while you wait, from €4.

Can I return or exchange something?

We make up tied flowers to order, so they can't be returned. But if a bouquet arrives damaged or wilted, get in touch within 48 hours and we'll make a new one or refund your money.

We'll exchange or refund an unused house plant in its original pot within 14 days of purchase. Details are in the Terms and cancellations section.

15 Terms

Cancellations, complaints and payment terms

Cut flowers don't last, so the deadlines for cancellations and complaints are shorter than for ordinary goods.

Cancelling an order

  • You can cancel an ordinary order free of charge up to 24 hours before the agreed delivery.
  • Made-to-order arrangements — bridal bouquets and wreaths — up to 48 hours before the date.
  • Once we've started making up the arrangement, the order can't be cancelled; the flowers are already cut and used.
  • A phone call or the contact form is enough to cancel; we'll send you confirmation in writing.

Complaints

  • Report problems with flowers within 48 hours of receiving them, ideally with a photo.
  • If your complaint is justified, we'll make a new bouquet or refund your money within 14 days.
  • Report problems with house plants within 14 days of purchase, in the original pot.
  • Complaints don't cover natural wilting after the normal vase life.

Payment and invoices

  • Prices are final and include VAT; delivery is added when you complete your order.
  • We only take a 30% deposit on wedding orders over €200.
  • We invoice business customers monthly with 14-day terms.
  • We give you a receipt on handover, and can print one later in the shop on request.

Data and privacy

  • We use your name, address and phone number only to deliver your order.
  • The recipient's phone number is used only to arrange the handover.
  • We don't share your details with third parties, except the courier delivering your order.
  • We keep orders for six years for tax purposes, then delete them.
