03 Your visit

How a repair works

Five steps from booking to collecting your car. The process is the same for every repair, even a small €12 job.

Step 01

Booking

Book a slot using the form on this page or by phone. You'll get a confirmation the same day.

Step 02

Drop-off

We note the fault, the mileage and anything else about the car. One technician takes the keys, and that's the person who keeps you updated.

Step 03

Inspection and price

We lift the car, confirm the fault and send you the price of labour and parts. We start work once you agree.

Step 04

The work

If we find another fault during the repair, we call you before we touch it. We keep the parts we replace.

Step 05

Collection

The invoice lists the work done and the parts used. We'll tell you what will need looking at at the next service.

    08 Terms

    Guarantee and repair terms

    A 24-month guarantee on labour, complaints resolved within 30 days and no extra work without your approval. The same applies to every repair.

    The price doesn't change without your approval
    Before the repair you get a written estimate for labour and parts. If another fault shows up during the work, we stop and call you. We don't continue without your approval.
    Inspection before the repair
    If the inspection shows we can't do the repair ourselves, we don't charge for it and we'll recommend where to go instead.
    Guarantee on labour and parts
    We guarantee our work for 24 months. Parts carry the manufacturer's warranty; its length is shown on the invoice.
    Replaced parts
    We keep the old parts and show them to you when you collect the car. If you want to keep them, tell us when you book.
    Complaints
    We accept complaints in person, by phone or through the contact form. We look at the car as a priority and resolve the complaint within 30 days at the latest.
    Customer's own parts
    We'll fit a part you bring in. We stand behind the fitting, not the part itself. Feel free to send us the part type before you buy it and we'll check it fits your car.
    Leaving the car after the repair
    You can leave the finished car on our premises for three working days at no charge. Longer stays can be arranged in advance.

    10 Bookings

    Cancelling and moving an appointment

    You can cancel or move a booking free of charge, even on the day of the repair. We hold your bay for 30 minutes.

    1. More than 24 hours aheadNo charge
      We'll cancel the booking or move it to another day at no charge at all. Just call or send a message through the contact form.
    2. Less than 24 hours aheadMoved to the next free slot
      We don't charge even then. We'll offer you the next free slot, though in the busy season that could be a week later.
    3. Running late on the dayWe hold it for 30 minutes
      If you know you won't make it, call us. We hold the bay for 30 minutes, then offer it to the next customer and find you another time.
    4. No-show without callingSlot released
      If you don't get in touch and don't turn up, we release the slot after 30 minutes. Next time you book, we'll ask you to confirm the day before.
    5. Cancelled by usWe call you straight away
      If a technician is off or an ordered part is late, we call you as soon as we know and offer the next available slot.
    6. Ordered partsThe deposit stays
      If we've ordered a part for your car and you cancel the repair, the deposit stays on the part. It's used up when you rebook the repair.

    How to cancel

    Call us during opening hours or send a message through the contact form. Include your name, the booking date and the make of your car so we can find it in the diary.

    Outside opening hours, feel free to send a message, even in the evening. We go through the diary every morning before the workshop opens.

    11 FAQ

    FAQ

    The ten things people ask us most often. If your question isn't here, call us or send a message through the form.

    01Do I need to book in advance?

    For repairs, yes — otherwise all the bays may be taken. Small things like topping up tyres, checking the pressure or filling up the screenwash we do without a booking if the workshop has a free moment.

    We try to take punctures straight away. Call before you come so we can tell you whether to come right over or an hour later.

    02How much will the repair cost?

    We can only give an exact amount after the inspection. The process is always the same: we lift the car, confirm the fault and send you an estimate for labour and parts. We only start work once you've approved the estimate.

    You'll find the prices of routine jobs in the labour price list on this page. These are labour prices; parts are charged according to actual use.

    03Can I bring my own parts?

    Yes, we'll fit them. In that case the guarantee covers the fitting, not the part. If the part fails, replacing it is charged as new work.

    Feel free to send us the part type before you buy it and we'll check it fits your car. It saves both of us a pointless strip-down.

    04How long does a tyre change take?

    A set of four wheels with balancing takes about 45 minutes. In March and April and again in October and November the tyre bay is often booked up a week ahead, so in the season it's worth booking early.

    If we store your set of wheels, we'll have it ready before you arrive and you'll wait less.

    05Can I wait at the workshop during the repair?

    Yes, by the entrance there's a waiting room with a view into the workshop, Wi-Fi and coffee. For jobs under an hour, most people wait on site.

    For repairs over two hours, we recommend leaving the car and coming back. We'll call you as soon as it's ready.

    06Do you have a courtesy car?

    We don't offer our own courtesy car. If the repair holds you up, we'll drive you to the town centre or the train station and pick you up again after the repair. Arrange it with us when you hand over the keys.

    07Will I lose the manufacturer's warranty if you service my car?

    No. We do scheduled services to the manufacturer's specified scope and record them in the service book or the digital service record. We use oils and parts of the specified quality.

    Keep the invoice with the exact breakdown of the work done — it's proof that the car has been properly maintained.

    08What if the fault comes back after the repair?

    Get in touch as soon as possible. We look at the car as a priority, and checking a repair under complaint is free of charge. If the fault is ours, we fix it under the guarantee.

    We resolve complaints within 30 days of receiving them at the latest and tell you in writing how we assessed them.

    09Can you store my tyres through the season?

    Yes, the store is in the same building as the workshop. We wash the set, label it with your name and registration number and put it on a rack. Storage for six months costs €29 per set.

    When you book a tyre change, we'll have the set ready before you arrive, so you'll wait less. You can collect your stored wheels any time — just call the day before.

    10Do you also service electric cars and hybrids?

    We do tyres, suspension, brakes, alignment and air con servicing on them. We don't work on the high-voltage drive system — we have neither the equipment nor the certification for it.

    If you need work on the battery or the drive system, we'll tell you as soon as you book so you don't make a wasted trip.

    14 Workshop

    Workshop, equipment and people

    A small four-bay workshop where the same person looks after your car from drop-off to collection.

    Premises and equipment

    The workshop has four bays. Three are for mechanical repairs; the fourth is kept for tyres so tyre changes don't hold up longer repairs. The tyre store is in the same building, so we can have your stored set ready before you arrive.

    • Two-post lift
    • Scissor lift
    • Wheel alignment rig
    • Diagnostics with on-road measurement
    • Air con station for R134a and R1234yf
    • Wheel balancer and tyre changer up to 20 inches
    • Tyre store

    Who looks after your car

    • Service advisor Takes in the car, notes the fault, prepares the estimate and calls you when something needs approving.
    • Mechanic Services, brakes, suspension, timing belts and clutches. Keeps the replaced parts with the car.
    • Diagnostics technician Takes measurements, traces electrical faults and prepares the measurement report for the invoice.
    • Tyre technician Tyre changes, balancing, puncture repairs and keeping track of stored wheel sets.
