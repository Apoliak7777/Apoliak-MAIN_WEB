FAQ

What you ask us most often

Do I need to book?

During the week, usually not — there are free lanes. On Friday and Saturday nights, yes — without a booking you often won't get a lane at all. Booking is free and without obligation; we just ask you to call if you can't come.

Do you have anything for kids?

Yes. Every lane has bumpers we can raise, so the ball can't go in the gutter and kids knock down pins instead of getting frustrated. We also have lighter balls and a ramp for the smallest ones. On Sundays, kids under twelve go half price.

How many people fit on one lane?

Six comfortably; with eight there's a lot of waiting. For bigger groups we recommend two lanes side by side — the booking calculator works it out for you.

Are bowling shoes compulsory?

Yes — you can't go on the lanes in street shoes: the surface would be damaged and, above all, it's slippery. Shoe hire is €2, and we disinfect the shoes after every use. You're welcome to bring your own bowling shoes, of course.

Can I smoke?

No — smoking and vaping aren't allowed anywhere inside. There's a covered area with an ashtray outside the entrance.

Do you have gaming machines?

No, and we never will. We're an entertainment centre for families and corporate events — bowling, pool, darts and a bar. Gambling doesn't belong here, and that isn't just marketing.
