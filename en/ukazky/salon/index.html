    Care

    Keeping your look between visits

    We'll tell you the same in the chair. It's written down here so you don't have to remember it at home.

    After colouring

    Wait 48 hours before you first wash your hair at home. The colour is still settling, and washing sooner rinses out the shine.

    Wash with lukewarm water — hot water opens the cuticle and the colour fades faster. Choose a sulphate-free shampoo.

    Chlorinated water and strong sun change the shade the most. Use a protective spray before the pool and on holiday.

    After a facial

    Don't wear make-up for the first 24 hours. Your skin is flushed and your pores are open, so make-up would clog them straight away.

    Skip the sauna, the pool and hard workouts for two days. Sweat and chlorine irritate freshly cleansed skin.

    After a chemical peel, wear sun cream even in winter, or the pigmentation will come back faster than it went.

    After a manicure or pedicure

    Wear gloves for cleaning and washing up. Cleaning products dull gel polish faster than anything.

    Don't use your nails as tools for opening things. Gel cracks from leverage, not from normal wear.

    Cuticle oil once a day makes it last a few days longer, and your nails won't look dry.

    FAQ

    What you ask us most often

    If your question isn't below, call or write to us. We reply outside opening hours too, just a little more slowly.

    Do I need to book in advance?

    Yes, we work by appointment only. When a slot frees up at the last minute, we call people who asked for that in the note when they booked.

    What if I'm late?

    We can manage if you're up to 15 minutes late, although we may have to shorten your treatment. If you're later than that, we'd rather move your appointment so the next client isn't kept waiting.

    How do I cancel or move an appointment?

    Call or text us. Changes are free up to 24 hours before, and we'll offer you a new appointment on the same call.

    How long does a big colour change take?

    For a colour correction, allow three hours. We'd like to see you a few days before for a consultation, to check the condition of your hair and agree on a realistic shade.

    Do you do a patch test?

    Before your first colour, yes. We do it 48 hours before your visit; it takes five minutes and is free.

    Can I come in just for a consultation?

    Yes. A hair consultation takes 20 minutes and is free. Book it like a normal appointment, and you'll leave with a care plan.

    Can I bring my own colour?

    We don't work with box dyes from the chemist, as we can't vouch for them. We can use your own professional colour, arranged in advance, but we can't guarantee the final shade.

    Do I pay in advance?

    No, you pay at the salon after your treatment. The exception is group bookings of three or more, where we ask for a €20 deposit.
