Prices

Legal fees

You choose how you'll be charged before we take on the matter, and it's part of the written terms of engagement. All amounts are shown excluding VAT.

Hourly rate

€120 / hour

The standard solicitor's rate. Trainee's work €80 an hour, criminal matters €140 an hour. Time is recorded in 6-minute units and every month you get a record describing each piece of work. Suits matters whose scope isn't clear yet.

Fixed monthly fee

from €600 / month

An agreed number of hours a month: €600 for 6 hours, €1,100 for 12 hours, €2,000 for 24 hours. Hours beyond the plan €100 an hour. Unused hours carry over to the next month, for up to two months.

Conditional fee

no win, no fee

In some debt and compensation claims we agree that our fee is only payable if the case succeeds. By law it can't be a share of the amount awarded — the rate is agreed in advance, together with who pays the court fees and costs.

Scale fee

under the court scale

Fees for individual steps under the District Court scale of costs, based on the value of the claim. Used mainly in court proceedings, where it is also the basis for awarding costs.

Confidentiality, personal data and cookies

How we handle what you give us — from the first message through the form to the shredding of the file.

01

Duty of confidentiality

It covers everything we learn in connection with your matter and continues after it ends. It binds the solicitor, the trainee and the office staff.

02

Handing over documents

We give a written receipt for originals and return them when the matter ends. We accept electronic documents on a USB stick at the office or by post to the office address.

03

Storing the file

The paper part of the file is kept in the firm's locked archive, the electronic part on an encrypted drive with a backup. Only the person working on the matter has access.

04

Personal data

We only process the data needed to provide the service and meet our legal obligations. Data from enquiries that go no further is deleted after 6 months.

05

Conflict of interest

Before taking on a matter we check whether we act for the other side in the same or a related matter. If we do, we won't act for you and will tell you without undue delay.

06

Communication

As standard we call the number you give in your enquiry and send letters to the agreed address. On request we send documents by email.

07

Cookies and tracking

This site uses no analytics or advertising cookies, stores no data in your browser and sends no data to third parties. That's why no cookie consent is needed.

08

Your data rights

You have the right to access your data, to have it corrected or erased, and to restrict its processing. Make your request in writing or in person at the office; we'll deal with it within 30 days.

09

How long we keep the file

We keep the file for 10 years after the matter ends. After that we shred the paper part and delete the electronic part, backups included.

FAQ

FAQ

Eight questions we get before the first meeting.

How is the time spent on my matter charged?

We record time in 6-minute units, that is, tenths of an hour. Every piece of work goes into the record with its date, description and duration — reading documents, drafting, phone calls, negotiations and attending hearings.

The record comes with the invoice, so you know exactly what you're paying for. We don't charge for a short follow-up question by email that takes a few minutes.

Do I have to come in person, or can everything be done remotely?

We can hold the consultation and our ongoing contact by video call. We need the authority to act on paper or with a qualified electronic signature; some documents need to be sworn before a commissioner for oaths.

We can exchange documents by post. We recommend meeting in person for matters where a lot of paperwork needs going through at once.

Do you take on matters outside Dublin?

Yes, we act before courts and public bodies throughout Ireland. In that case travel at €0.45 per kilometre and travel time at half the hourly rate are added to the fee.

For matters further away we estimate the number of trips in advance, so you know what to expect.

When do I get an invoice and when is it due?

With an hourly rate we invoice monthly, by the 5th of the following month, payable within 14 days. Fixed fees are invoiced in advance on the 1st of the month.

For matters expected to take more than 20 hours we ask for a 30% payment on account, which is set against the first invoice.

What if I change my mind?

You can withdraw your instructions at any time without giving a reason. We only bill for the work done up to then and hand over the file, including originals, without delay.

If a deadline is running, we warn you about it in writing so you don't miss it while moving to another solicitor.

Do you provide legal services free of charge?

No. The firm charges fees agreed in advance. People who can't afford a solicitor can apply to the Legal Aid Board in civil matters or ask the court for legal aid in criminal cases.

If we find that your matter falls into this category, we'll tell you at the very start.

How quickly can you start if a deadline is running?

We deal with urgent matters with a running deadline first — we can usually offer a consultation within 48 hours. In that case we need to know the date you received the decision or letter at the very first contact.

For express handling within 48 hours we add a 25% surcharge to the hourly rate.

Can you guarantee we'll win?

No. The outcome depends on the evidence, the other side and the court's decision, and no firm can promise it. We agree on the approach, the scope of work and what can realistically be achieved in your matter.

We tell you in writing about the risks and the possible costs of losing, including the other side's costs, before anything is filed.
