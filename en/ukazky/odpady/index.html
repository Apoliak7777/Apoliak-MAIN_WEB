FAQ

What you ask us most often

What can't go in a skip?

Hazardous waste — asbestos, paint, oil, solvents, batteries, tyres and electrical items. We do take asbestos, but under special arrangements, by agreement and in our own packaging. If hazardous waste turns up in an ordinary skip, the whole load is charged at the highest rate.

Do I pay by volume or by weight?

By weight. Transport is a fixed price depending on the skip size, and disposal is charged by the weight on a certified weighbridge. That's why rubble is cheaper than you'd expect — and why it doesn't pay to order a small skip and pack it to the brim.

Do I need a permit when the skip is on the road?

If it stands in a public place — a footpath, a car park or the road — yes, you need a skip permit from the council. We'll arrange it for you; it takes two to five working days. On your own property or driveway you don't need a permit.

Can you take it away without a skip?

Yes — for smaller amounts, a flatbed truck comes with two people and we load by hand. It's cheaper than a skip for one van-load of furniture or a few bags of rubble.

How quickly can you deliver?

Usually within 24 hours, and often the same day if you order by 14:00. We can do express within three hours if we have a truck free — for a surcharge. On Saturdays we run until lunchtime.

Do you pay for scrap metal straight away?

Yes, when you drop it off at the yard. We pay by bank transfer and need photo ID — that's how we keep a record of every purchase.
