Levels based on the Common European Framework

It doesn't matter how many years you've studied a language — what matters is what you can do in it.

  • A1BeginnerYou can introduce yourself, order food and say where you're from.
  • A2ElementaryYou can handle a hotel, shopping and everyday chat about family and work.
  • B1IntermediateYou can get by on holiday and in a simple work discussion.
  • B2Upper intermediateYou can manage a meeting, emails and a film without subtitles.
  • C1AdvancedYou speak fluently, even about specialist topics, and with ease.
  • C2Near-nativeYou get jokes, irony and regional nuances.

FAQ

What you ask us most often

I studied English for eight years and I know nothing. Where will you put me?

We hear this almost every week. Years at school tell us nothing — what matters is what you can do in the language today. Most people like this end up in A2, where they quickly fill in what they were missing, and then they progress very fast, because they have more in their heads than they think.

What if I miss a lesson?

You can make it up in another group at the same level — just let us know a day in advance. You can make up at most five lessons like this per term. With one-to-one lessons you can move the time for free if you do it 24 hours ahead.

Is the textbook included?

No, you buy the textbook separately — it costs about €24 and it's yours to keep. Copies, worksheets and all the extra materials are included and we don't charge for them separately.

Do you do online lessons?

One-to-one, yes; group lessons, we'd rather not. We tried it, and in an online group of six, half the people stop talking. If you can't make it in, we offer one-to-one or a course for two online.

Will I get a certificate?

When you finish the term, you get a certificate showing the level you've reached. It isn't an internationally recognised certificate like Cambridge or Goethe — we can prepare you for those, but you sit the exam at an accredited centre.

Can I join a course during the term?

Yes, if there's a place in the group and the level fits. You only pay for the remaining lessons. The first two lessons can be hard to keep up with, so your teacher will send you what the group has covered so far.
